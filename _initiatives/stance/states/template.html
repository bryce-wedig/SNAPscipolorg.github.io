How to use this resource

This webpage houses the responses to TODO questions crafted by the TODO Stance on Science team. To find candidates' responses for each election, you can filter by tag, race, district, and party. Available tags include: TODO Tag 1, TODO Tag 2, TODO Tag 3.

Only official candidates who will appear on ballots were contacted.

To find your district and who is on your ballot, please visit the TODO state voter resource.

Candidates were contacted in TODO Month Year (with a follow-up to those who did not initially respond) and given the opportunity to answer all questions. Only the responses of candidates who answered the questions are included. None of the candidates' responses were modified in any way before being published on this resource page.
