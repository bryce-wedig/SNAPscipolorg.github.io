Stance on Science

Stance on Science is SNAP’s nationwide initiative to ask political candidates about their positions on science policy-relevant issues. Each state will ask 2 standardized questions along with designing 2-6 of their own questions, guided by SNAP recommendations, and all responses will be hosted on our website. Our goal is to be a trusted source of non-partisan information to the public regarding candidates’ stances on key science policy issues!
