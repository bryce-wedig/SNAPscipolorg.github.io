The SNAP Congressional District Visits initiative helps early-career scientists meet with their Members of Congress during the August recess. By shifting advocacy from DC-based fly-ins to hometown offices, District Visits lower barriers to participation, build local connections, and emphasize that science is not an abstract enterprise but a vital part of every congressional district.

August 2026 District Visits

We're doing it again! SNAP is organizing a new round of congressional district visits in August 2026. This year, our primary objective is to discuss the proposed OMB federal financial assistance rule (Docket OMB-2026-0034) and its potential negative impacts on scientific researchers and the public.

Training and Preparation

  • Primary Training: August 12, 2026 at 2:00 PM ET, co-led with AAAS
  • Backup Training: August 17, 2026 at 6:00 PM ET

SNAP will assist participants by scheduling 15–30 minute meetings with congressional offices, hosting training sessions, and providing materials such as district-specific one-pagers to help members convey their points effectively.
