## Ready to Get Involved?

Whether you're part of an established science policy group or an individual advocate, you're welcome here. <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSclm6qDeLHbwRk4QPuZR34x77xx5dlKz3tHuHitBdWAiWe5vg/viewform?usp=header">Complete the Interest Form</a> to join our mailing list and stay updated through our newsletter! You can also indicate whether you'd like to be added to our Slack, where you can connect with fellow advocates, organize initiatives, and access shared resources!
